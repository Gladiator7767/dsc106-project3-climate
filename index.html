Read the comparison correctly

A recent-history baseline. +2°C of warming means 2°C above 1995–2014 in that model and region. It is not a Paris Agreement threshold.

Conditional projections. The scenarios describe different emissions assumptions. Neither has an assigned probability. A larger scenario gap is not a probability or a policy-effect estimate.

A small model sample. CanESM5, IPSL-CM6A-LR and MPI-ESM1-2-LR; one run each. Their min–max range is not a confidence interval or the full range of possible futures.
